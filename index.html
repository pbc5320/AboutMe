Research

Semantic Prosody Detection in Historical Corpora

ARL Walker Graduate Assistantship · Fall 2026 – present · Python

I am building and refining a Python algorithm that detects semantic prosody: the evaluative meaning a word acquires from the company it keeps. The tool is applied to historical corpora of 18th- and 19th-century Gothic fiction and British newspapers, tracing how evaluative meaning formed and circulated during the rise of the British Empire. It is designed to adapt to multiple languages, with applications to contemporary media and geopolitical framing.

Dividing the Indivisible: Modeling Nuclear Issue-Indivisibility Within Negotiations

Master's thesis · Penn State · 2026 · R

Tests whether perceived issue indivisibility, rather than structural factors such as regime type or power asymmetry, predicts negotiation failure. Uses ordered logistic regression on 30 post-Cold War nuclear negotiations.
