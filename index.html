About

Security, conflict, and the language that frames them

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Political Science at Penn State, advised by Dr. Jared Edgerton, and a Walker Graduate Assistant at Penn State's Applied Research Laboratory. I hold a B.S. in Security and Risk Analysis and a Master of International Affairs (Middle Eastern Studies), both from Penn State, and I am a CyberCorps Scholarship for Service Scholar.

My research asks why negotiations fail, how states use repression and violence, and how language shapes the way we understand conflict. I increasingly work at the intersection of political violence and language: I am building computational tools that detect how words acquire evaluative meaning, and I am interested in how online extremist movements recruit and radicalize, and how their language evolves to evade content moderation.

Research Interests

Current Focus Areas

Language and Meaning

Semantic prosody, collocation, and corpus methods: how words take on evaluative meaning from the company they keep, and how that meaning spreads.

Online Extremism

Online recruitment into radical extremism, and the coded language extremist communities use to evade content moderation.

Political Violence

Cross-national work on the relationship between autocratic governance and the lethality of terrorism across more than 100 countries.

Government Repression

How governments use coercion against their own populations, and what that means for stability and violence.

Nuclear Negotiations

Why do some nuclear bargaining efforts collapse? My thesis tests whether perceived issue indivisibility matters beyond regime type and power asymmetry.

Domestic Politics and War

How domestic institutions and political incentives shape decisions to fight, bargain, or settle.

State Failure

The conditions under which state capacity erodes, and how that connects to conflict and insecurity.

Comparative Politics

Cross-national and regional comparisons of nuclear identities, regime types, and political repression, with a focus on the Middle East.
